Horalix Joins NVIDIA Inception to Advance AI Echocardiography

NEWS | July 16, 2026 | Sarajevo, BiH

Horalix joined NVIDIA Inception, gaining access to NVIDIA’s global AI startup ecosystem as it advances AI-assisted echocardiography software.

Short answer

Horalix has been accepted into NVIDIA Inception, NVIDIA’s global startup-support program for companies developing artificial intelligence and advanced technology. The acceptance, publicly announced on 16 July 2026, gives Horalix access to technical resources, training, selected partner benefits, and opportunities within NVIDIA’s international AI startup ecosystem as the company continues developing AI-assisted echocardiography software.

Horalix joins NVIDIA Inception

Horalix has joined NVIDIA Inception, a global program designed to support startups developing artificial intelligence, data science, accelerated computing, and other advanced technologies.

The milestone was reported by Klix.ba, which identified Horalix and Roboticks as two startups from Bosnia and Herzegovina accepted into the program.

For Horalix, joining NVIDIA Inception represents recognition of the company’s work at the intersection of:

  • Artificial intelligence
  • Medical imaging
  • Echocardiography
  • Clinical workflow software
  • GPU-accelerated computing
  • Digital health

What is NVIDIA Inception?

NVIDIA Inception is a free startup-support program created by NVIDIA for companies building products with artificial intelligence and advanced computing.

According to NVIDIA, program members may receive access to benefits such as:

  • Technical training and developer resources
  • Opportunities to connect with NVIDIA experts
  • Preferred pricing on selected NVIDIA products
  • Partner offers and cloud-related benefits
  • Co-marketing resources
  • Startup and investor networking opportunities
  • Access to parts of NVIDIA’s global technology ecosystem

Unlike a traditional accelerator, NVIDIA Inception does not require startups to join a fixed cohort or surrender company equity simply to participate in the program.

Acceptance into NVIDIA Inception does not mean that NVIDIA has invested in, certified, or clinically validated Horalix. It means that Horalix has been accepted as a member of NVIDIA’s startup-support ecosystem.

Why NVIDIA Inception is relevant to Horalix

Horalix develops AI-assisted software for analyzing cardiac ultrasound studies and supporting structured echocardiography reporting.

Medical imaging artificial intelligence can require substantial computing resources during both model development and clinical processing. Graphics processing units, commonly known as GPUs, are widely used to train and run the deep-learning models behind image classification, segmentation, measurement extraction, and video analysis.

Horalix’s technology may process multiple forms of information from an echocardiography study, including:

  • Cardiac ultrasound video
  • Individual image frames
  • Heart-chamber boundaries
  • Anatomical key points
  • Cardiac measurements
  • Changes across the cardiac cycle
  • Structured clinical information

Membership in NVIDIA Inception can support Horalix as it works to improve the performance, reliability, and scalability of these AI systems.

Building AI-assisted echocardiography software

Echocardiography is a cardiac imaging method that uses ultrasound to examine the heart’s chambers, valves, movement, and function.

Although image acquisition happens during the examination, significant work may remain afterward. Cardiologists and sonographers often need to identify relevant views, perform measurements, review cardiac structures, and organize the findings into a clinical report.

Horalix is building its CardiologyAI platform to assist with this post-acquisition workflow.

The platform is designed to:

  • Analyze echocardiography studies
  • Extract more than 50 cardiac measurements
  • Produce structured, report-ready outputs
  • Reduce repetitive manual processing
  • Support standardized documentation
  • Preserve clinician review and final approval

Horalix is not positioned as an autonomous diagnostic system. The software supports the clinical workflow, while qualified medical professionals remain responsible for verifying outputs and making patient-care decisions.

A milestone for Bosnia and Herzegovina’s AI ecosystem

The acceptance of Horalix and Roboticks into NVIDIA Inception also reflects the growing visibility of technology startups from Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Both companies were publicly connected to the Techstars Sarajevo Founder Catalyst ecosystem, which has supported founders working to develop internationally scalable products from Sarajevo.

Horalix’s acceptance is particularly relevant because medical AI companies from smaller startup markets face significant barriers when entering international healthcare and technology ecosystems. These can include access to specialized technical knowledge, computing infrastructure, healthcare partners, investment networks, and international market visibility.

Programs such as NVIDIA Inception can help reduce some of these barriers by connecting startups with resources and organizations that would otherwise be difficult to access.

What NVIDIA Inception can help Horalix accomplish

As Horalix continues developing and deploying its software, NVIDIA Inception may provide support in several important areas:

  • Optimizing AI inference performance
  • Improving GPU utilization
  • Scaling medical imaging workloads
  • Accessing technical education
  • Learning from NVIDIA’s developer ecosystem
  • Connecting with technology partners
  • Building international startup visibility
  • Preparing the product for larger clinical deployments

The practical value of the program will depend on how Horalix uses these resources throughout its product-development and commercialization journey.

What does joining NVIDIA Inception mean for hospitals?

Hospitals should not interpret program membership as a substitute for clinical evidence, regulatory compliance, data-security evaluation, or local validation.

For healthcare organizations, Horalix must still demonstrate that its software is reliable, secure, usable, and appropriate for the intended clinical workflow.

NVIDIA Inception can support the technical development of the platform, while Horalix’s hospital pilots, validation activities, evidence disclosures, and clinician feedback remain essential to evaluating the product itself.

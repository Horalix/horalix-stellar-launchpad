Horalix Clinical AI Workflow Solutions

Horalix builds modular clinical AI workflow products that target high-friction operational bottlenecks in medical imaging. Each solution is designed to reduce manual post-acquisition work, produce structured outputs, and keep clinicians in control of review and final sign-off.

The primary clinical priority is CardiologyAI — AI-assisted echocardiography workflow for faster measurement extraction and report preparation. Additional modules for pathology and radiology workflows are in development.
